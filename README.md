# Robin Connor Schramm

My overarching goal is to enable meaningful interaction, visualization, and information dissemination through novel multimodal interfaces and technologies. My multidisciplinary research in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is deeply rooted in experimental methods and the design, implementation, and evaluation of interactive systems. A significant portion of my work involves building functional prototypes for realistic use contexts and studying them empirically to understand how people interact with emerging technologies beyond controlled lab conditions. My current work focuses on creating and evaluating novel interfaces that can support future forms of human-AI interaction.

For a full list of my work, have a look at my [website](https://schrammr.github.io/).
