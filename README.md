# Robin Connor Schramm

I am a PhD student in Human-Computer Interaction at RheinMain University of Applied Sciences, conducting my research in collaboration with Mercedes-Benz Tech Innovation, where I focus on applications of in-car Augmented Reality for passengers.

My research in Human-Computer Interaction is dedicated to designing, building, and studying interactive systems for real-world use. I am particularly interested in how immersive and context-aware interfaces can be developed and evaluated in ecologically valid settings, combining artifact-driven research with simulation and field studies. A significant part of my work explores how interactive AR systems can enrich travel experiences, while also investigating how simulation can serve as a methodological bridge between early prototyping and deployment in everyday environments.

For a full list of my work, have a look at my [website](https://schrammr.github.io/).
